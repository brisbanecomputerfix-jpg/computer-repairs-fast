How to Migrate Telstra BigPond & Optus Email to Outlook 365 (Step-by-Step)

Safely Migrating Away from Dying Australian ISP Email

For over two decades, hundreds of thousands of Queenslanders have relied on Telstra BigPond (@bigpond.com, @bigpond.net.au) and OptusNet (@optusnet.com.au) email addresses. However, Australian telcos are progressively outsourcing or decommissioning legacy mail infrastructure. Migrating your decades of archived emails into Microsoft Outlook 365 or Gmail protects your tax records, invoices, and family memories permanently with zero data loss.

The Step-by-Step Safe Migration Roadmap

  1. Export Historical Offline PST Archive: In Outlook Classic, navigate to File > Open & Export > Import/Export > Export to a file > Outlook Data File (.pst). Select the root account folder and ensure 'Include subfolders' is checked. Save this backup file to an external USB drive.
  2. Provision Modern Cloud Mailbox: Create a modern, independent Microsoft 365 / Outlook.com or Google Workspace account with two-factor authentication (2FA).
  3. Import Archive & Re-Establish Folder Hierarchy: In the new profile, import the .PST file. All historical folders (Inbox, Sent Items, Archive, Receipts, Family) populate seamlessly into cloud storage.
  4. Configure Automated Server-Side Forwarding: Log into Telstra Webmail or Optus Webmail and set up automatic forwarding to your new address. Set up an auto-responder notifying contacts of your new email.
  5. Update Critical Service Logins: Systematically update your email on banking (CommBank, NAB, ANZ), myGov, utility providers, and medical clinics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Will my BigPond emails disappear if I change internet providers?

If you disconnect your Telstra home internet service, Telstra generally charges an ongoing annual mailbox fee or closes the account after a grace period. Migrating to an independent provider ensures you never lose access.

Q. Can you migrate my BigPond emails on my computer at my Brisbane home?

Yes! We attend your home, back up your complete email database, configure Outlook, and verify send/receive functionality on your PC, iPad, and phone.

Q. What if I don't know my BigPond webmail password?

We assist you through the Telstra identity verification workflow to reset your master password before performing the export.
