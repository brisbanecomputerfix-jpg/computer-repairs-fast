Why Dual Monitors Flicker or Say 'No Signal'

Multi-display issues usually stem from three distinct bottlenecks:

  • DisplayPort Multi-Stream Transport (MST) Overload: Trying to drive dual 4K 60Hz displays through a basic 5Gbps or 10Gbps USB-C port without Display Stream Compression (DSC).
  • macOS MST Limitations: Apple Silicon Macs do NOT support standard DisplayPort MST daisy-chaining. Plugging two cables into a PC dock will merely mirror the second screen on a Mac unless DisplayLink compression software is installed.
  • Uncertified Cables: Using generic low-spec HDMI cables or passive DisplayPort converters that drop signal sync when graphics power states change.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Why does my second monitor display 'No Signal' when plugged into my dock?

This is frequently caused by video bandwidth caps or outdated dock firmware. We test cable protocols and configure proper DisplayPort Alt-Mode configurations on-site.

Q. Can I run two external monitors from a standard MacBook Air?

Yes! While base M1, M2, and M3 MacBook Air models natively support only one external monitor, we configure DisplayLink dual-display setups that bypass this hardware limit.

Q. Do you supply certified Thunderbolt 4 cables and adapters?

Yes. Our mobile van carries certified 40Gbps Thunderbolt cables, active DisplayPort adapters, and 100W USB-C GaN power supplies.
