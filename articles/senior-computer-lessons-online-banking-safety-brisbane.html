Senior Computer Guide: Safe Online Banking, Passwords & Scam Protection

Patient In-Home Computer Coaching for Brisbane Seniors

Technology should make life easier, not more stressful. For seniors and retirees in Brisbane, keeping up with constant computer updates, mobile apps, and confusing banking security requirements can feel overwhelming. Computer Repairs Fast provides patient, friendly, 1-on-1 in-home computer lessons across Brisbane. We explain everything in plain English, never rush, and write down clear instructions in your own notebook.

Top 5 Essential Skills Covered in In-Home Lessons

  • Safe Online Banking: How to log in safely, verify you are on the genuine bank website (CommBank, NAB, ANZ, Westpac), and transfer funds to family with confidence.
  • Spotting Fake Scam Calls & SMS Texts: Learning the golden rule: genuine banks, Telstra, and the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) will NEVER text you a clickable link or demand urgent payment via gift cards.
  • Simple Password Management: Moving away from unsafe scraps of paper to a secure, easy-to-use password system that you won't forget.
  • Sharing & Organizing Family Photos: How to transfer pictures from your smartphone or digital camera onto your computer and email them to grandchildren.
  • Video Calling Family on FaceTime, Zoom & WhatsApp: One-touch video calling with crisp audio and video to stay connected with loved ones anywhere in the world.

Free Brisbane Community & Government Computer Classes

Looking for Free Community Tech Classes in Brisbane?

In addition to our private 1-on-1 in-home lessons, Brisbane seniors have access to fantastic free resources:

  • Brisbane City Council Libraries: Free 1-on-1 tech appointments across 33 branches (Brisbane Square, Carindale, Toowong, Chermside, Indooroopilly).
  • Be Connected (beconnected.esafety.gov.au): Free online lessons for Australians aged 50+ on myGov, banking, and spotting scams. Helpline: 1300 795 897.
  • U3A Brisbane (u3abrisbane.org.au): Volunteer-run technology classes for seniors in the Brisbane CBD and suburbs.
  • IDCARE (idcare.org): Free identity and cyber support helpline on 1800 595 160 if you ever feel scammed.
