How to Create Free Email Aliases on Google & Cloudflare Custom Domains

Multiple Inboxes for Zero Extra Cost

You do not need to pay $6 to $15 every month for separate email accounts like info@, sales@, and billing@. You can create up to 30 free aliases on Google Workspace, use the free Gmail '+' trick, or use Cloudflare Email Routing to forward unlimited custom domain email addresses directly into your free personal Gmail inbox for $0.

The 3 Best Ways to Setup Free Email Aliases

  1. Cloudflare Email Routing (100% Free Custom Domain Email):

    In Cloudflare > Email Routing, enable routing. Create custom addresses (e.g., contact@yourdomain.com) and route them directly to your personal @gmail.com inbox. Cloudflare handles spam filtering and MX records for zero dollars.

  2. Google Workspace Alternate Email Aliases (30 Free per User):

    If you pay for one Google Workspace account: Log into Google Admin (admin.google.com) > Users > Click user > Add Alternate Emails. Add info@, support@, and accounts@. All emails arrive in your main inbox.

  3. The Free Personal Gmail '+' Filter Trick:

    Add +category to your address (e.g., john+newsletter@gmail.com). In Gmail Settings > Filters, automatically route those emails into specific folders or bypass the inbox.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can I reply using my custom domain alias from free Gmail?

Yes! In Gmail Settings > Accounts and Import > 'Send mail as', add your custom domain address using a free SMTP relay like SendGrid, Resend, or Brevo.

Q. Will my customers know I am using an alias?

No. The email headers and display name show your professional alias address (e.g., Support Team ).

Q. Can I create catch-all email forwarding in Cloudflare?

Yes! Cloudflare supports catch-all routing, meaning ANY email sent to your domain (even typos like random123@yourdomain.com) is safely delivered to your designated personal inbox.
