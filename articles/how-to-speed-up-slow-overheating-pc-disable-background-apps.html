How to Speed Up a Slow, Overheating Computer & Disable Background Apps

Why Modern Computers Get Sluggish & How to Fix Them

A sluggish, loud computer is usually plagued by two bottlenecks: 1. Software Bloat (dozens of useless background apps hogging memory and CPU cycles on startup), and 2. Thermal Throttling (cooling radiators choked with dust and dried thermal paste forcing processors to throttle down to a crawl). Disabling startup bloatware and completing a physical clean-out can double your machine's speed.

How to Disable Startup Bloatware in Windows 11 & 10

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager.
  2. Click on the Startup apps tab (the speedometer or list icon on the left panel).
  3. Review the list of programs set to launch automatically with Windows.
  4. Right-click and Disable non-essential applications: Spotify, Discord, Cortana, Adobe Creative Cloud, Steam, Epic Games, and manufacturer helper utilities. (Leave your audio drivers and Windows Security enabled).
  5. Reboot your computer. Boot time will typically drop from 90 seconds to under 15 seconds!

The Best Free Unbloating Software for Windows

  • Revo Uninstaller Free: Standard Windows uninstallers leave behind thousands of orphaned registry entries and temp files. Revo performs an aggressive post-uninstall deep scan to purge all traces of stubborn software.
  • O&O ShutUp10++ (Free): A lightweight portable utility that disables invasive Windows background tracking, Cortana telemetries, and advertising services with one click.
  • CrystalDiskInfo (Free): Checks the health of your internal SSD or hard drive to ensure sluggishness isn't caused by failing flash memory sectors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Will disabling startup apps stop them from working when I open them?

Not at all! It only prevents them from launching silently in the background when you turn on your PC. When you double-click their icon, they open normally.

Q. Can you upgrade my old mechanical hard drive to an SSD in Brisbane?

Yes! Swapping an old mechanical spinning hard drive for a high-speed Solid State Drive (SSD) is the single biggest upgrade you can perform, making an 8-year-old laptop feel faster than when it was new.

Q. Why does my laptop fan roar loudly when just browsing the web?

Dried factory thermal paste cannot transfer heat to the copper heatsink, causing the processor to overheat even under light loads. Fresh thermal paste drops temperatures by 15°C to 25°C.
