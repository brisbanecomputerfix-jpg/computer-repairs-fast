Which VPN Is Best? How to Browse the Web Without Digital Footprints

Choosing a Genuine Privacy VPN (Not Marketing Hype)

Most mainstream VPN review sites are owned by the very VPN conglomerates they review. For real privacy, technicians recommend Mullvad VPN (flat €5/mo, requires no email or personal details, accepts cash, audited RAM-only servers) and Proton VPN (Swiss jurisdiction, strong free tier). Avoid free VPNs that monetize by selling your DNS queries and browsing logs to data brokers.

Top VPN Recommendations by Use Case

  • For True Anonymity & P2P Downloads: Mullvad VPN (€5 / Month Flat)

    Mullvad does not ask for your name, email, or password. It generates a 16-digit account number. You can pay via credit card, crypto, or literally mail cash in an envelope to their Swedish office. Operates diskless RAM servers that cannot log traffic.

  • For Everyday Security & Streaming: Proton VPN

    Based in Switzerland outside the 14-Eyes surveillance alliance. Offers a legitimate unlimited-bandwidth free plan and Secure Core multi-hop servers for journalists and privacy advocates.

  • What to Avoid: Free VPN apps on Google Play/App Store that inject tracking SDKs, and conglomerate-owned VPNs that have previously cooperated with logging subpoenas.

How to Surf Without Your ID Being Collected

  1. Encrypted Private DNS: Configure DNS-over-HTTPS (DoH) using NextDNS or Quad9 (9.9.9.9) to stop your ISP from seeing which websites you visit.
  2. Disable WebRTC Leaks: WebRTC can reveal your real residential IP even when a VPN is running. Brave and Firefox have native toggles to disable WebRTC IP leakage.
  3. Browser Isolation: Use one hardened browser (Brave) for shopping and browsing, and a separate browser for logging into sensitive personal accounts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does a VPN make me 100% anonymous?

No. A VPN conceals your IP address and encrypts network traffic. But if you log into your personal Google or Facebook account, your identity is recognized through account authentication and browser fingerprinting.

Q. Why is Mullvad cheaper than other VPNs?

Mullvad does not spend millions on celebrity sponsorships or affiliate marketers. They charge a flat €5 per month with zero long-term subscription traps.

Q. Can Australian banks detect when I am on a VPN?

Yes. Australian banks flag logins from foreign VPN IP addresses as potential fraud. When doing online banking, pause your VPN or use a local Australian server node.
