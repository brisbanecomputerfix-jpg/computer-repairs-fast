Common Gaming PC Distress Symptoms We Fix

  1. Instant Shutdown Under Load: The PC runs fine on desktop or YouTube, but the moment you launch Cyberpunk or Call of Duty, it clicks and powers off instantly. This is the classic symptom of a failing power supply (PSU) tripping over-current protection (OCP) during modern GPU transient power spikes.
  2. AIO Liquid Cooler Pump Failure (100Â°C CPU): Loud rattling, buzzing, or gurgling sounds from the pump block accompanied by CPU temperatures idling at 90Â°C to 100Â°C and extreme thermal throttling. Over 2-3 years, liquid coolant permeates or sediment clogs microscopic cold plate fins.
  3. GPU Artifacts & nvlddmkm.sys Driver Crashes: Green checkered squares, pink horizontal lines, or games crashing to desktop with display driver crash errors indicate unstable GDDR6X video memory or cracked solder balls under heavy GPU sag.
  4. RAM XMP / EXPO Instability: Random KERNEL_SECURITY_CHECK_FAILURE or MEMORY_MANAGEMENT blue screens caused by sub-optimal DDR5 sub-timings or uncalibrated memory controller voltages.

Our Workshop Stress Testing & Benchmarking Routine

We don't guess. We mount your components on our diagnostic bench and run exhaustive stress loops:

  • Cinebench R23 / 2024: Isolates CPU core thermals, power draws, and throttling thresholds.
  • FurMark 2 & 3DMark Time Spy / Steel Nomad: Pushes GPU core, memory junction temperatures, and 12VHPWR cable stability to maximum limits.
  • MemTest86 Pro: Verifies every individual bit of DDR4/DDR5 system memory.
  • Oscilloscope PSU Rail Inspection: Checks +12V, +5V, and +3.3V rail voltage ripple under dynamic loads.
