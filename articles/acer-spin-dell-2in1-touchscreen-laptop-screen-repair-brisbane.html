Cracked Glass vs LCD Black Ink Bleed: What's the Difference?

When an Acer Spin or Dell 2-in-1 laptop hits the floor, two distinct types of damage can occur:

  • Top Glass Fracture Only: The picture looks normal and vibrant, but spiderweb cracks run across the surface, touch input fails, or fingers catch on sharp edges.
  • Internal LCD Shatter: You see black liquid blobs, vertical green/pink lines, or half the screen is completely white or distorted. This occurs when the underlying liquid crystal layer is punctured.

Because these screens are manufactured as a unified laminated unit, both faults are completely cured by installing a fresh factory assembly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Can you fix a Dell Inspiron 2-in-1 if the corner of the aluminum lid is bent?

Yes. We gently reshape bent aluminum casing corners using specialized precision chassis tools so the new replacement display sits perfectly flush.

Q. Why did touch stop working on my Acer Spin after a minor drop?

The capacitive touch matrix connects to the motherboard via a separate micro-flex ribbon cable. Drops can dislodge or tear this cable, or crack the invisible touch sensor film.

Q. How long is the warranty on replacement screens?

All our replacement screen assemblies include a comprehensive 12-month warranty covering display defects, touch response, and color accuracy.
