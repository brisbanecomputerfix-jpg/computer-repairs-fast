The 3 Most Common Ways Students Crack MacBooks

Apple MacBooks have an extremely tight toleranceâ€”less than 0.3 millimeters of clearance between the display glass and the keyboard keys when closed. The top three causes of accidental cracks are:

  1. Camera Privacy Covers: Cheap plastic sliding webcam shutters purchased online are 1.2mm thick. Closing the lid with one installed concentrates pressure directly onto the glass, causing an instant spiderweb fracture radiating from the webcam notch.
  2. Objects Left on the Keyboard: A misplaced pen, highlighter, paperclip, or single AirPod left on the palmrest when shutting the lid will crush the lower glass chin bezel.
  3. Overstuffed Backpacks: Carrying a MacBook in a backpack pressed tightly against heavy textbooks, water bottles, and gym sneakers bends the aluminum lid, causing internal liquid crystal ruptures without any exterior glass scratches.

Q. Why did Apple refuse to fix my screen under AppleCare or Consumer Law?

Apple classifies cracked screens, internal glass bleeding, and pressure fractures as 'accidental user damage', which is not covered by the standard 1-year warranty or Australian Consumer Law.
