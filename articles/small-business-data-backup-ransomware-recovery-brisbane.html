Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is OneDrive or Dropbox enough to protect our business from ransomware?

No. Most ransomware strains silently encrypt your local OneDrive or Dropbox folder. The sync client immediately syncs those encrypted files up to the cloud, overwriting your good data across all office PCs.

Q. What should our staff do immediately if ransomware is detected?

1. Disconnect all network cables and turn off Wi-Fi immediately on every office PC. 2. Do NOT reboot or power off the infected machine. 3. Call our emergency business response line immediately on 0401 148 654.

Q. Can you help our business comply with the Australian Privacy Act?

Yes. We implement enterprise AES-256 encryption, access control audits, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) to ensure compliance with Australian Privacy Principles (APP).
