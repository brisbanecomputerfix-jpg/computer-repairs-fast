Telstra & NBN Bank Scam on Your Computer? Immediate Emergency Action Plan

Immediate Emergency Action: 3 Things To Do Right Now

1. Disconnect From the Internet Immediately: Unplug the network cable or turn off your Wi-Fi router. 2. Do Not Turn the Computer Back On: If remote tools like AnyDesk or TeamViewer are running, shutting down cuts off the hacker's active tunnel. 3. Call Your Bank From a Separate Phone: Notify your bank's fraud department to freeze accounts before callers execute unauthorized wire transfers.

How the 'Telstra / NBN Hacker' Scam Operates in Australia

Across Brisbane and Queensland, thousands of residents—particularly retirees and seniors—receive calls from criminals pretending to be from Telstra Technical Support, the NBN Co, or Commonwealth Bank / ANZ Fraud Prevention. The script is almost identical:

  1. The caller claims your home internet is 'hacked' or broadcasting illegal traffic.
  2. They instruct you to download remote support software such as AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport, or UltraViewer.
  3. Once granted access, they blank out your monitor screen or open black command windows to simulate 'scanning' while stealthily accessing your browser saved passwords, email, and online banking.
  4. They often stage a fake refund, claiming they deposited $5,000 into your account by mistake and demanding you buy gift cards or transfer money back.
