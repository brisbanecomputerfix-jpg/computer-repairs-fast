How to Transfer All Files, Photos & Settings From Old PC to New PC

Moving to a New PC Without Losing a Single File

Bought an exciting new Windows 11 computer in Brisbane but stressed about how to transfer thousands of family photos, Chrome bookmarks, tax documents, and email accounts? Using generic cloud sync or USB copying often misses hidden browser profiles, Outlook archive files, and software product keys. A professional bit-level migration replicates your entire digital life with zero data loss.

Checklist: The 7 Hidden Items Most People Forget to Transfer

  • Browser Autofill & Saved Passwords: Chrome, Edge, and Firefox credentials, auto-complete forms, and bookmark bars.
  • Local Outlook .PST Files: Stored in %localappdata%\Microsoft\Outlook, containing decades of emails and contacts not stored on the mail server.
  • Desktop Accounting & Tax Databases: Local MYOB (.myo), Quicken/Reckon (.qbw), or e-tax PDF archives.
  • Custom Font & Photo Editing Libraries: Lightroom catalogs, RAW photo caches, and custom presets.
  • Network Printer & Scanner Drivers: Pre-configured network port bindings for seamless printing.
  • Windows Sticky Notes & Downloads Folder: Quick notes and important downloads stored on the local drive.
  • Software License Keys: Extracting digital product keys (Office, Adobe, CAD) prior to decommissioning the old machine.
