## 🚑 Brand & Service Concept: "The Computer Ambulance"

ComputerRepairsFast (CRF) operates on an emergency rapid-response model:
1. **We Come To You:** Mobile technicians travel directly to the customer's home or office anywhere across Brisbane. Customers never have to unplug cords or lug heavy equipment around.
2. **Fixed $80 Diagnostic Fee:** Full hardware and software diagnostic to isolate the exact failure. No hidden surprises.
3. **$0 Call-Out / Travel Fees:** Travel across metro Brisbane is 100% included in the fixed diagnostic.
4. **Upfront Fixed Quotes:** All repair costs (screen replacements, SSD speed upgrades, scam cleanups) are quoted with client approval before any work begins.
5. **Same-Day Rapid Fixes:** Standard laptop screens replaced in 1 to 3 hours; remote bank scams neutralized immediately.
6. **Senior & Pensioner Friendly:** Ultra-clean red and white high-contrast UI, 19px readable fonts, patient plain-English explanations with zero technical jargon, and a 10% senior community discount.
