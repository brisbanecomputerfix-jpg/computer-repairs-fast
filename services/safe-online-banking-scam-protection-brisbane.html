Frequently Asked Questions

Safe Online Banking & Scam Protection Consultation Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Will you ever ask for my banking PIN, password, or account balance?

Never! We strictly adhere to privacy codes. When setting up secure login windows or passwords, our technician turns away or steps back while you enter your private credentials.

Q. How do I know if an SMS from my bank is genuine or a scam?

Banks in Australia will NEVER send you a text message containing an active clickable link asking you to log in or update details. We teach you how to verify genuine communications and install tools that block fake phishing links.

Q. What should I do if a loud alarm sounds on my screen saying my PC is infected?

Do NOT call the 1800 number on the screen. It is a fake browser popup designed to trick you into granting criminals remote access. Simply hold down your power button to turn off the computer and call us.
