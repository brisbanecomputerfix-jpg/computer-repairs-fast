Motherboard Micro-Soldering Repairs Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Why choose micro-soldering instead of replacing the entire motherboard?

Replacing an entire motherboard can cost $800 to $1,500—often more than the laptop is worth. In reality, motherboards usually fail because of a single 50-cent capacitor or power chip that shorted out. By replacing the individual microscopic component under a microscope, we save you 60% to 70% of the cost.

Q. What is your success rate on motherboard micro-soldering?

Over 85% of non-tampered dead motherboards and liquid-damaged boards are successfully revived on our bench.

Q. What warranty is provided on micro-soldered motherboards?

Every motherboard repair includes a 90-day parts and labor warranty.
