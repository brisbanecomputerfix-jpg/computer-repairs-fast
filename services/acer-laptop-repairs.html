Acer Laptop Repairs Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Why does my Acer Aspire casing separate at the corner when I open the lid?

This is a classic failure mode on Acer Aspire laptops: the brass screw nuts break free from the brittle plastic casing. We rebuild the foundation using reinforced epoxy so you don't have to replace the whole housing.

Q. Can you upgrade the speed of a slow Acer Aspire or Swift?

Yes! Most older Acer laptops came with slow mechanical hard drives. Upgrading to a modern NVMe M.2 SSD makes the laptop 5x to 10x faster with instant booting.

Q. How long does an Acer screen replacement take in Brisbane?

Standard 15.6" screens for Acer laptops are kept in stock and can be fitted same-day in just 1 to 2 hours.
