Urgent Protection Protocol

What to Do Right Now to Protect Your Data

The actions you take in the first 10 minutes determine whether files can be retrieved or permanently lost:

01 🛑

1. UNPLUG THE DRIVE IMMEDIATELY

If your mechanical hard drive is clicking, grinding, chirping, or dropped, unplug the USB cable right now. In mechanical drives, magnetic platters spin at up to 7,200 RPM while read heads hover just 3 nanometres above the surface. A damaged head in contact with spinning platters physically scrapes away magnetic data layers, creating rotational score rings that destroy files forever.

02 🚫

2. AVOID THE "FREEZER TRICK" & OPENING SCREWS

Do NOT follow internet myths claiming you should freeze your hard drive. Moisture in Brisbane's humid air will condense on the internal platters as frost. When powered, the liquid causes catastrophic head crashes. Never take the screws off a hard drive outside an ISO-5 cleanroom—single dust particles act like boulders beneath read heads.

03 ⚠️

3. NEVER RUN CHKDSK OR FREE RECOVERY APPS

Windows will often pop up asking: "Do you want to scan and fix removable disk?" Never click Yes. Windows CHKDSK attempts to write repairs directly to failing sectors, overwriting corrupted file indexes and permanently destroying file names and directory trees.

04 🔬

4. LAB-GRADE WRITE-BLOCKED HARDWARE CLONING

We connect your drive to dedicated hardware imagers with physical write-blockers. The imager bypasses operating system timeout freezes, controls read head positioning, selectively skips bad head assemblies, and creates a raw sector-by-sector clone onto our secure drives before extracting your files.

Recovery Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions: Data Recovery

Clear, plain-English answers to common questions about file retrieval in Brisbane.

When you bring or dispatch your storage drive to us, we connect it to dedicated hardware diagnostic imagers to inspect the health of the read/write heads, PCB board, firmware, and file allocations. Our fixed $80 diagnostic fee is credited directly into your final recovery quote, so you never pay extra for testing.

We adhere to strict confidentiality protocols. Data recovery is performed entirely on offline, non-networked recovery benches. Your recovered files are never uploaded to the cloud or viewed by unauthorized staff. Once you have received your files, we maintain an encrypted temporary backup for 14 days in case you lose the new drive, after which it is permanently purged.

Because failing drives cannot safely be written to again, your recovered data must be saved onto a new destination storage device. You can provide your own working external hard drive or high-capacity USB stick, or we can provide a brand-new Western Digital or Seagate portable drive at true retail market cost ($85 – $120).

Logical recoveries (deleted files, RAW filesystems) are often completed same-day or within 24 hours. Drives with degraded physical sectors that require gentle multi-pass sector-by-sector cloning typically take 24 to 72 hours.
