Frequently Asked Questions

Overheating & Loud Fan Noise? Fixed Today FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Why is my computer fan running so loud and hot?

Over time, Brisbane dust, pet hair, and lint form a thick 'carpet' on the inside of the heatsink radiator fins, blocking exhaust airflow. Simultaneously, factory thermal paste dries out into chalk, preventing heat from transferring from the CPU to the cooler. We disassemble, clean, and repaste.

Q. Can overheating permanently damage my laptop?

Yes. Sustained heat above 90°C degrades micro-solder balls under the GPU and CPU and causes internal lithium batteries to swell. Timely servicing prevents catastrophic logic board failure.

Q. Can you do this service at my home?

Yes, our mobile technician carries precision antistatic vacuums, compressed air, and premium thermal pastes to service your computer right on your table.
