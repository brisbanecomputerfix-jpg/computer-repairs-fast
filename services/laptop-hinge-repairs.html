Broken Laptop Hinge? Repaired Without Buying New FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Why did my laptop hinge break?

Modern laptops are engineered with powerful steel hinges bolted into very thin, brittle plastic housings using tiny brass threaded inserts. Over time, hinge friction increases, and the torque rips the brass nuts straight out of the plastic. We solve this by loosening the hinge tension and reconstructing the mounting base with high-strength structural resin.

Q. Do I need to buy a brand-new screen or casing?

No! In 90% of cases, we reconstruct the damaged plastic foundation, saving you hundreds of dollars compared to buying rare overseas replacement parts.

Q. Is the repaired hinge as strong as the original?

It is actually stronger than original! Our metal-reinforced polymer bond has a much larger surface area than the fragile factory plastic pins.
