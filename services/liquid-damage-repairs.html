Immediate Emergency Action

Spilled Liquid? Follow These 4 Steps Right Now

Doing the right thing in the first 5 minutes can save you over $1,500 in replacement costs.

🔌

1. Cut Power Immediately

Hold down the power button for 10 full seconds until all lights switch off. Unplug the charging cable right away. Never plug the charger back in to "test if it still turns on"—19 volts passing through wet circuits burns holes straight through logic boards.

🚫

2. Avoid The Rice Myth

Do NOT put your laptop in a bag of uncooked rice. Rice dust and starch enter the USB ports, cooling fans, and keyboard switches. Worse, rice does not stop chemical oxidation or dissolve dried milk and sugar solids that continue corroding underneath microchips.

⛺

3. Tent Position Over Towel

Open the laptop screen to approximately 90 degrees and place it keyboard-downwards on a dry towel in an inverted "V" (tent) position. This allows gravity to pull liquid away from the motherboard rather than letting it pool onto sensitive components.
