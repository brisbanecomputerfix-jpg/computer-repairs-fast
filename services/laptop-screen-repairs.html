Diagnostic Knowledge

How to Know If Your Laptop Screen Is Broken

Before replacing parts, we isolate whether the issue is the LCD panel, video cable, or graphics chip:

01 💔

Cracked Inner LCD vs Top Glass

Often the outer plastic or glass feels smooth to the touch, but beneath the surface you see black spiderweb ink patches or kaleidoscope lines. This means the liquid crystal substrate has ruptured. The panel cannot heal and must be replaced.

02 🔦

The "Flashlight Test" (Backlight Inverter Failure)

If your screen looks completely pitch black but your laptop power light is on and fans are running, shine your smartphone's flashlight directly at an angle against the screen. If you can faintly see your desktop icons or mouse cursor, your display is generating an image, but the LED backlight circuit has failed.

03 📺

Vertical Lines & The External HDMI Test

Plug your laptop into a TV or external computer monitor using an HDMI cable. If the picture looks crystal clear on your TV, your graphics card (GPU) and motherboard are 100% fine—only your laptop's LCD panel or internal video ribbon cable is faulty!

04 ⚡

Screen Flickering When Moving The Lid

If your screen blinks, flashes, or turns off only when you tilt the laptop display backwards or forwards, the eDP video ribbon cable passing through the hinge has become pinched or frayed. We inspect and replace damaged cables or re-seat the 30-pin connector.
