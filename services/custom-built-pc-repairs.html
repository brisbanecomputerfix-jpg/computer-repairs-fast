Custom Built PC Repairs Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. My custom PC fans spin but there is no picture on the monitor. What is wrong?

This is a classic 'No POST' state. It is usually caused by bad RAM seating, corrupt BIOS, failing power supply rails, or a dead graphics card. We bring bench diagnostic tools to your home to isolate the exact failed component within 30 minutes.

Q. Can you test a power supply on-site without damaging my other components?

Yes, our technicians carry digital ATX PSU load testers that verify all +12V, +5V, +3.3V, and -12V rails before connecting power to your motherboard.

Q. Do you build or upgrade custom PCs for flight simulators and 3D rendering?

Yes, we build, re-case, and upgrade high-end workstation and gaming systems tailored to your exact budget.
