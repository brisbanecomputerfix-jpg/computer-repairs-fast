Engineering Bench Diagnostic

How We Diagnose & Stabilize High-Performance Rigs

No guessing. We stress-test each component individually on our specialized test bench:

01 ⚡

GPU Black Screens & Transient Power Spikes

Modern high-tier GPUs (NVIDIA RTX 3080, 3090, 4080, 4090 and AMD RX 7900 XTX) can draw instantaneous millisecond power spikes exceeding 600 Watts. If your Power Supply Unit (PSU) cannot suppress these transient voltage spikes, its internal Over-Current Protection (OCP) will trigger, instantly cutting power to your graphics card and throwing black screens while the fans roar at 100%.

Bench Isolation: We test your graphics card under synthetic FurMark loads on a clean gold-rated ATX 3.0 bench supply, isolating whether the card's VRAM has degraded or if your power supply requires a dedicated 12V-2x6 rail upgrade.
02 💧

AIO Liquid Cooler Pump Failure & Micro-Fins Clogged

All-in-One (AIO) liquid coolers from Corsair, NZXT, Cooler Master, and Lian Li have an average lifespan of 3 to 5 years. Over time, coolant permeates out through rubber tubing (permeation loss), air bubbles accumulate inside the CPU cold-plate block, and galvanic corrosion flakes clog the copper micro-fins, causing the impeller pump to grind and fail.

Symptom: CPU spikes to 95°C – 100°C within 60 seconds of turning on the PC. We replace failed units with top-tier 240mm/360mm AIOs or zero-maintenance high-performance dual-tower air coolers (Thermalright Peerless Assassin / Noctua).
03 📉

BSOD Memory Errors & DDR5/DDR4 XMP/EXPO Instability

Enabling Intel XMP or AMD EXPO high-speed memory profiles can cause memory controller instability, leading to random Blue Screens of Death (BSOD) with stop codes like IRQL_NOT_LESS_OR_EQUAL, MEMORY_MANAGEMENT, or PAGE_FAULT_IN_NONPAGED_AREA.

MemTest86 Validation: We run boot-level MemTest86 stress suites across individual memory channels, tune sub-timings, calibrate SoC voltage, and flash the latest AGESA / microcode BIOS updates to achieve 100% rock-solid gaming stability.
04 💡

Motherboard Debug LED POST Troubleshooting

When a gaming PC powers on with RGB lighting but shows zero picture on your monitor, the motherboard diagnostic LEDs provide the critical clue:

• CPU LED Solid Red: Bent socket pins (LGA1700/AM5), mounting pressure uneven, or BIOS microcode incompatibility with newer generation processors.
• DRAM LED Solid Amber: Unseated RAM, dirty gold contacts, or bad channel routing.
• VGA LED Solid White: GPU PCIe lane failure, missing 8-pin power plug, or legacy CSM display handshake issue.
• BOOT LED Solid Green: Boot drive partition corrupted or missing UEFI bootloader.
05 ❄️

Thermal Paste Degradation & Case Airflow Optimization

Factory thermal pastes dry out and "pump out" over heat cycles, leaving bare silicon spots on your CPU and GPU dies. Combined with negative case air pressure pulling Brisbane dust through unfiltered seams, your system slowly suffocates.

Phase-Change Solution: We use industrial Honeywell PTM7950 phase-change pads or high-viscosity Thermal Grizzly Kryonaut, optimize intake-to-exhaust fan curves, and balance positive case pressure for whisper-quiet thermal performance.
06 🤝
