ASUS Laptop Repairs Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Can you replace ASUS ZenBook OLED screens in Brisbane?

Yes, we stock and source factory-grade ZenBook OLED panels, ensuring 100% DCI-P3 color reproduction, true blacks, and full brightness control.

Q. My ASUS ROG Zephyrus is reaching 96°C when gaming. Can you fix it?

Yes. ASUS ROG laptops use liquid metal from the factory which can oxidize or pump out over time. We clean off oxidized liquid metal, treat the copper cold plate, and apply fresh thermal compound.
