Computer Insurance Damage Reports Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. What is included in your computer insurance assessment report?

Our formal report includes our registered Australian Business Number (ABN), photographic evidence, component serial numbers, technical diagnosis of cause (e.g. liquid ingress, kinetic impact, electrical surge), an itemized breakdown of repair costs, and a formal recommendation on whether the device is repairable or a total write-off.

Q. Are your reports accepted by all Australian insurance companies?

Yes! Our assessment reports are accepted by RACQ, Suncorp, Youi, Allianz, QBE, AAMI, NRMA, and credit card insurance providers.

Q. Can the insurance assessment fee be claimed back?

In almost all cases, your insurer will reimburse our assessment report fee as part of your overall approved claim payout.
