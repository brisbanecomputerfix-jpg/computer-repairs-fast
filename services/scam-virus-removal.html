If A Scammer Was On Your Screen: 4 Steps Right Now

Take these critical precautions immediately before our technician arrives.

🔌

1. Disconnect The Internet

Unplug the Ethernet cord or turn off your computer's Wi-Fi immediately. If you are unsure how, simply turn off your home NBN modem or router at the wall power socket. This severs the scammer's remote connection instantly.

🏦

2. Call Your Bank (From Your Phone)

If you logged into internet banking while the caller was connected, call your bank's official 24/7 fraud hotline using your mobile phone. Request a temporary security freeze on outward transfers until your PC is audited.

🚫

3. Do Not Call The Pop-Up Number

Never call the 1800 or mobile numbers flashing on red warning screens. Legitimate security providers like Microsoft or Apple will never lock your screen and demand you call a phone number.
