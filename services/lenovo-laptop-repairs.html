Frequently Asked Questions

Lenovo Laptop Repairs Brisbane FAQs

Clear answers to your technical and repair questions.

Q. Can you fix broken hinges on Lenovo Yoga laptops without replacing the whole screen?

Yes! Lenovo Yoga laptops frequently suffer from snapped brass screw standoffs inside the lid. We reconstruct and re-anchor the mounting brackets with industrial resin and brass inserts, saving you $300+ over buying a whole new display lid.

Q. Why is my Lenovo ThinkPad not charging via USB-C?

This is a common issue with ThinkPad T and X series models caused by a firmware bug or shorted Thunderbolt controller. We test the power delivery rails and replace damaged power ICs on the motherboard.

Q. Do you repair Lenovo Legion gaming laptops that overheat?

Yes, we disassemble the thermal module, deep clean the fin stacks, and apply high-performance non-conductive thermal paste or PTM7950 phase change pads.
