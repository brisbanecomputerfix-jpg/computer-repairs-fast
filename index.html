💡 Quick Cheatsheet

Where to Find Your Computer's Model Number in 10 Seconds

🍏 Apple MacBooks

Turn the MacBook upside down. Look for the small text near the hinge: Model AXXXX (e.g. A2337, A2159). Or click  > About This Mac.

💻 Dell Laptops

Look on the underside sticker for the 7-character alphanumeric Service Tag (e.g. J7K2M91). You can also press F2 on startup.

🖥️ HP, Lenovo & ASUS

Look for "Product Number" or "MTM" on the bottom sticker. Alternatively, press Win + R, type msinfo32 and look at System Model.

🎮 Custom Gaming PCs

Note your motherboard model (or CPU/GPU if known). If the PC boots, press Ctrl + Shift + Esc > Performance tab to see CPU, RAM, and GPU.

Still not sure? Just text a quick photo to 0401 148 654 and we will identify it for you!

💡 Accurate Pricing Guide

What to Provide Your Technician for an Itemised Quote

Getting an accurate, fixed quote with a complete breakdown of replacement parts cost, professional labour, and turnaround timeframe is simple when you have the right details ready. Provide these 5 items to our Brisbane technicians via SMS, WhatsApp, or our booking form for an instant, transparent quote.

1 🏷️

Exact Model or Serial Number

Check the underside label for your laptop's Model Number, Dell Service Tag (7 alphanumeric digits), Lenovo Serial / MTM, or Apple Model AXXXX (e.g. A2337). For desktop PCs, note the motherboard model or CPU/GPU specs.

Why Technicians Need It: Laptop screens have different pin counts (30-pin vs 40-pin eDP), refresh rates (60Hz vs 144Hz), and bracket mounts. Your serial tag verifies exact warehouse parts in 30 seconds.
2 ⚡

The Trigger Event & Symptoms

Describe what was happening when the fault occurred: Did the laptop drop from a desk, was coffee/water spilled on the keys, did it freeze during a Windows update, or did you smell electrical burning near the charger port?

Why Technicians Need It: Identifies whether the fault is an isolated component (like a broken screen or dead battery) or requires motherboard micro-soldering and ultrasonic cleaning.
3 📸

1–2 Clear Smartphone Photos

Take a clear photo of the underside barcode sticker, and if the display is damaged, take a photo with the screen powered on. For software crashes, snap a photo of any Blue Screen (BSOD) stop code.

Why Technicians Need It: Seeing the display powered on reveals if only the outer glass digitizer is broken or if the inner LCD matrix has ruptured, ensuring an accurate part quote upfront.
4 ⏱️

Urgency Level & Timeframe

Let us know if you need Emergency Same-Day Mobile Dispatch (for home businesses, urgent work deadlines, or exam week), 24-Hour Express Turnaround, or standard scheduled workshop drop-off.

Why Technicians Need It: We stock 80%+ of common screens, batteries, and NVMe SSDs in Brisbane trade hubs. Knowing your timeline lets us allocate and reserve stock immediately for your repair.
5 🔒

Data Sensitivity & Backup Status

Indicate whether you have critical unbacked files (MYOB/Xero accounting, family photos, school assignments) and if Windows BitLocker or Apple FileVault is active on the machine.

Why Technicians Need It: Ensures our technicians place your storage drive into write-blocked hardware cloning mode first, guaranteeing 100% data preservation before any physical stress testing.
