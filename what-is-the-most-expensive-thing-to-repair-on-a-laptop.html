What Is the Most Expensive Thing to Repair on a Laptop?

Written by Senior Computer Technicians in East Brisbane, West End & Toowong.

📍 In Brisbane and unsure whether to repair or replace?

Don't guess. We come to your door or you can drop off at our East Brisbane or West End workshops. For a fixed $80 diagnostic fee (no travel charge), we inspect the machine and give you a flat-rate quote. Call 0401 148 654.

When your laptop suddenly stops turning on or has an accident, the big question is always: “Is it going to cost more than the computer is worth?”

Here is the honest breakdown from our workbench of what costs the most to fix on modern laptops, and when it makes financial sense to repair vs replace.

1. The Motherboard (Logic Board) — The Most Expensive Part

The motherboard is the main circuit board that connects everything inside the computer. On almost all modern laptops (especially MacBooks, Dell XPS, and slim HP ultrabooks), the processor (CPU), graphics chip (GPU), and memory (RAM) are soldered permanently onto this board.

  • Full Board Replacement: Can cost between $450 and $1,200+ (often half the price of the laptop).
  • Our Micro-Soldering Alternative: In many cases, we don't have to replace the whole board. Our technicians find the tiny shorted chip, capacitor, or power fuse and replace it for $180 – $260, saving your laptop at a fraction of the price.

2. Specialized Touchscreens & OLED Displays

While standard non-touch Full HD laptop screens start from $230 to $330, specialized display assemblies can be significantly more expensive:

  • Touchscreen School Laptops (Acer Spin, HP x360, Lenovo Yoga): Usually cost $350 to $430 because the glass digitizer touch layer is factory-bonded to the display panel.
  • OLED & 4K Touch Displays: Premium panels (found on Dell XPS OLED, HP Spectre, ASUS Zenbook Pro) run higher and are quoted upfront based on panel market rates.
  • Apple MacBook Retina Upper Display Assemblies: Typically $290 – $550+ depending on the model generation.

3. Broken Laptop Hinges & Top Housing Frame Sockets

Many customers assume a loose or broken hinge just needs a tightened screw. In reality, modern laptops house heavy steel hinges inside thin plastic shells. When the steel hinge stiffens from dust and age, opening the laptop rips the threaded brass anchor sockets straight out of the plastic palmrest.

Fixing this properly requires sourcing and installing a brand-new top housing / palmrest frame assembly, which generally costs between $250 and $320.
