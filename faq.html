🔧 Laptop Hinges, Broken Casings & Liquid Spills

Modern laptop hinges are made of stiff steel, but they are anchored into tiny brass threaded nuts molded into thin plastic casing. Over time, hinge friction increases from dust. Opening the lid generates immense leverage that rips the brass nuts straight out of the plastic. Simply tightening or gluing the plastic rarely lasts.

A proper, permanent hinge repair requires replacing the palmrest / top housing frame assembly or rear display cover where the anchor sockets reside. This complete structural rebuild generally costs between $250 and $320.
